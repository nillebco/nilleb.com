Privacy: audience measurement

Applies to nilleb.co, pressto.app and the other nillebco sites that load analytics.nilleb.com. Last updated 19 November 2026.

These sites count their visits with a small analytics service that nillebco wrote and runs itself. It exists to answer “how many people read this page, and where did they come from?” — nothing else.

What is recorded

  • the address of the page you opened, without its query string;
  • the page that linked to it (the referrer), also without query string;
  • your browser and operating system family, and your browser's preferred languages;
  • your country, looked up on the server from your IP address with a local database (IP Geolocation by DB-IP, CC BY 4.0);
  • an anonymous visitor identifier, computed from the above and your IP address with a random value that is replaced every day and then deleted. It lets the statistics count one visitor once per day; it cannot be traced back to you or linked to your visits on another day.

What is not

  • No cookies, and nothing stored on your device: there is no banner because there is nothing to consent to.
  • Your IP address is never stored, and never sent to anyone else.
  • No tracking across sites, no advertising, no profiles, nothing sold or shared.

Where and how long

The data stays on a server in the European Union (Hetzner, Nuremberg, Germany) and is deleted automatically after 13 months.

Saying no

Turn on Global Privacy Control or “Do Not Track” in your browser and no visit is recorded. Blocking analytics.nilleb.com works too.

Who is responsible

nillebco EURL, Ivo Bellin Salarin, Lyon, France — ivo@nilleb.com. The measurement relies on legitimate interest (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)) and follows the conditions under which the CNIL exempts audience measurement from consent. Questions or objections by email; you can also complain to the CNIL.

En bref

Mesure d'audience sans cookie, réalisée par nillebco sur ses propres serveurs (UE) : adresse de la page sans paramètres, page d'origine, navigateur, langue et pays. L'adresse IP n'est ni conservée ni transmise ; l'identifiant de visiteur change chaque jour. Données supprimées après 13 mois. Pour vous y opposer : activez Global Privacy Control ou « Do Not Track », ou écrivez à ivo@nilleb.com.
